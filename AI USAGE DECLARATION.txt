AI USAGE DECLARATION

Project: ShohojSeba

Generative AI tools, primarily ChatGPT were used during the development of this project as a supporting tool.

AI assistance was used for:
- Code suggestions and debugging
- UI/UX improvement suggestions
- Explanation of errors and implementation concepts
- Assistance with testing and troubleshooting
- Documentation preparation

All AI-generated suggestions were reviewed, modified where necessary, integrated into the existing project, and tested during development.

The final implementation and submitted project were verified by the project developers.
